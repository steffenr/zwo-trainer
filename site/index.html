Three ways to ride

Follow a plan. Pick a session. Find your FTP.

The app opens on what's next. Everything else is one click away.

01 · Plans

A folder becomes a plan

Any folder with a weekly schedule turns into a plan. zwoTrainer keeps your place, tracks done and skipped days, and always has the next session ready.

 02 · Workouts

Your whole library

Search, filter by length and zone, sort by TSS, star favorites. Every row shows the workout's shape so you know what you're in for.

 03 · FTP tests

Know your number

Five built-in tests with live pacing, a plausibility check and one click to apply your new FTP — zones update in watts right away.
The ride screen

Built to be read at 170 bpm.

Three panels and a chart — nothing that doesn't help you hold the number. Big, high-contrast readouts in pure black or pure white, zone colours you already know.

  • Live: power with target, cadence, speed, heart rate and a watt gauge.
  • Current & next segment: target %, watts, time left — prepare before the change.
  • Workout chart: your power, cadence and HR traced over the planned blocks.
  • Intensity: nudge every target ±1 % or ±10 % from the keyboard or your controller.

Light or dark — follows your system, or pick one in the settings.

Plans

Show up. Press start. Done.

Drop a week-by-week folder of .zwo files into your library and it's a plan. Each workout unlocks in order, skips are one click and reversible, and a Markdown file in the folder becomes the plan's description.

  • Progress per plan — switch plans and come back where you left.
  • Week / day layout with duration, zone and TSS for every session.
  • End early? Save it as done, count it as skipped, or discard it.
FTP tests

Pacing that keeps you honest.

All-out efforts release ERG so you ride on gears — real or virtual. While you suffer, zwoTrainer shows your running average, the FTP it adds up to and a warning when you start too hot.

  • Ramp, Ramp Lite, 20 & 8 minute, Power Profile — plus test files from your own library.
  • Fair results: an effort only counts when you actually completed it.
  • W/kg and VO2max estimates, test history and an FTP trend.
After the ride

Your ride, everywhere it belongs.

Every ride is saved as a FIT file and goes to Garmin Connect — named after the workout, not “Indoor Cycling” — and on to Strava from there. Intervals.icu uploads directly; COROS gets a ready-made import.

  • Time, average power, NP, HR, cadence, energy at a glance.
  • Garmin sign-in on Garmin's own page, MFA supported — your password never touches the app.
  • Riders sync through iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Nextcloud.
Works with your pain cave

Pair it once. Ride every day.

Devices are remembered per rider and reconnect on launch and after dropouts.

Smart trainers

Any Bluetooth FTMS trainer — Wahoo KICKR, Zwift Hub, Tacx, Elite, Saris, JetBlack… with ERG and flat-road simulation.

Power & heart rate

Bluetooth power meters and any heart-rate strap or watch that broadcasts HR.

Virtual shifting

Zwift Ride, Click and Play controllers, keyboard or on-screen buttons: a 24-speed virtual drivetrain with real road physics.

Your platforms

Garmin Connect (and Strava through it), Intervals.icu and COROS. Plain FIT export for everything else.
